How Bene uses your health data

Bene reads health data from Health Connect only so it can show you your own recovery, sleep and training information on this device.

What Bene reads

  • Steps and active calories
  • Sleep sessions and sleep stages
  • Heart rate, resting heart rate and heart rate variability
  • Respiratory rate, oxygen saturation and body temperature
  • Exercise time and workouts
  • Weight and height

What Bene does with it

Everything is processed on your phone and stored in Bene's local storage. Bene does not write anything back to Health Connect, has no account, and does not upload your health data to any server.

What you control

You can grant or revoke any of these permissions at any time in Health Connect settings. Revoking a permission stops Bene from reading that type of data; data already stored locally stays until you clear it in Bene's settings.

Bene is a local-first app. Deleting the app deletes the data Bene stored.
