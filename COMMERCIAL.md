# Commercial licensing

Bene is **source-available**, not OSI open source.

## Free use

Personal, hobby, research, educational and other noncommercial use is free under the
[PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE). You may read, run, modify and share
the source for those purposes.

## Commercial use requires a paid licence

No commercial use is permitted until a separate written commercial licence is in place.
That includes, without limitation:

- selling Bene or a product built from it;
- distributing it as part of a paid app, bundle or service;
- hosting it as a paid or ad-supported service;
- using it in production for a business, consultancy or client;
- incorporating it into a commercial product, even if Bene itself is not the thing
  being sold.

A commercial licence is available on a **revenue-share basis**: a percentage of gross
revenue attributable to the product or service that incorporates Bene, reported
quarterly. A flat fee or buyout can replace the revenue share where that is simpler.
The rate and floor are agreed in the signed licence, not by this page.

To request a licence, contact the maintainer through the repository hosting profile.
Include the product, the commercial model, and an estimate of annual revenue.

This page is a licensing policy, not legal advice. Consult a lawyer before signing or
relying on a commercial arrangement.
