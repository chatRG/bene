# Bene — design brief

## The name
**Bene** — 4 letters, 2 syllables, lowercase, Latin *well*. Sayable, short, and already the name on the app.

## What it is
A local-first health and recovery app for iOS and Android. Sleep, strain, recovery and biological age. No network calls, no cloud; the data lives on the phone and is backed up by the user.

## Audience
Someone who already trains and already tracks. They are not looking for motivation or a coach personality. They want a quiet instrument that tells them the truth about their own body and then gets out of the way.

## Adjectives
1. **Quiet** — it does not shout, notify, or gamify.
2. **Honest** — no invented numbers; the maths is shown.
3. **Composed** — calm, never urgent, never clinical-cold.

## Where it must work
- App icon at 16 px in a crowded home screen
- Favicon in a browser tab
- One colour, embroidered or foiled, no gradients
- Reversed on ink
- Next to OS health apps (Apple Health, Google Fit) without looking like a knock-off

## Category look, and what to avoid
Health apps cluster on: hearts, pulse lines, rings, leaves, shields, blue gradients, rounded sans letterforms. Also **a lowercase initial** is the default app-icon move — it says nothing and belongs to no one.

Off-limits: pulse lines, hearts, leaves, rings-of-three, medical crosses, human silhouettes.

## Strategy
Bene measures **baseline and return**: the body drifts, then comes back. The promise is not "more" or "faster"; it is *the known point you return to*. That is a **baseline** — a horizontal reference line — and a body settling onto it.

So the mark is an **abstract mark**, not a letterform: horizontal reference line + a form that settles onto it. Two ideas, one shape, no letters.

## Concepts to build
- **A — Equilibrium**: a heavy baseline and a disc resting on it. Mass above a known line; the line is the measurement, the disc is the body.
- **B — Return**: the baseline plus an arc that leaves and lands back on it, one end open. Drift and return.
- **C — Span**: the baseline plus two uprights of different heights carrying a single bar — your measured range, read between two points.

All three: black first, `viewBox 0 0 256 256`, geometric construction, exact angles, no gradients, must survive 16 px.

## Deliberate departures from the brief in this round
The wordmark is **not** being revised. Allo lowercase at weight 500 already reads calm and honest, and a type change would be taste, not strategy.
